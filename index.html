FAQ

Questions suppliers ask first.

Short answers, using only what’s on this page. Anything else — team@dominiq.site.

What is Dominiq?

Dominiq is AI compliance software for Tier 2 and Tier 3 defense subcontractors. It classifies ITAR and CUI files, maps evidence to the 110 NIST SP 800-171 requirements, and vets suppliers against the clauses you flow down to them.

Who is Dominiq built for?

The operations manager or office lead at a small defense manufacturer — a machine shop, electronics builder or specialty supplier — who owns the SPRS score without a dedicated compliance department.

CMMC Phase 2 is paused. Do we still need this?

Only Phase 2 third-party certification was suspended, on 13 July 2026. Phase 1 self-assessments and annual SPRS affirmations remain in force, and DFARS 252.204-7012 still requires NIST SP 800-171 and 72-hour incident reporting.

How does Dominiq handle ITAR technical data?

Dominiq is designed to run inside your own Microsoft 365 GCC High or Azure Government tenant through read-only connectors, so files are not copied out and the Dominiq team does not need access to them.

What does Dominiq produce for an assessment?

An SPRS estimate scored line by line and tied to evidence, a System Security Plan and POA&M with every open item tracked against the 180-day closeout window, an inventory of every ITAR and CUI file, and a flow-down ledger of your suppliers.

What happens when Dominiq spots a possible CUI incident?

It starts the DFARS 252.204-7012 reporting clock and lays out the steps: contain, preserve images for at least 90 days, and report through DIBNet.

Is Dominiq a C3PAO or legal counsel?

No. Dominiq is software that organizes compliance evidence; it does not issue CMMC certifications and it is not legal or export-control advice.
